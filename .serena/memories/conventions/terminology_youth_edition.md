# Terminology Standard: Youth Edition MVP (青春版)

- All simplified baseline / MVP scopes across Dinkoko ERP, DanLee CRM, TanJee Accounting, and UI channels are officially designated as **「青春版 (Youth Edition MVP)」**.
- The term "悲觀" (pessimistic) is deprecated and permanently removed.
- Strategy pairing: **「青春打底，樂觀擴充」** (Youth Edition MVP Baseline vs. Optimistic Full Expansion).
